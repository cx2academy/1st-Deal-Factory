The Exact Process

The entire wholesaling process simplified. No fluff. No extra steps.

Step 1: Pick the Right Market

Beginners fail when they choose bad markets. You want: investor activity, fixer properties, cash buyers. A good market makes everything easier.

Step 2: Generate Seller Leads

Your job is simple: start conversations. Primary methods: Facebook Marketplace, cold calling, SMS outreach. You're not looking for perfect sellers. You're looking for people open to a conversation.

Step 3: Start the Conversation

Your goal is NOT to sell. Your goal: sound normal, build comfort, see if selling is even an option. If they're open → continue. If they're not → move on.

Step 4: Qualify the Seller

Once they're open to selling, find out: why they'd sell, condition of the property, their price expectations. You're not convincing them. You're collecting information.

Step 5: Run the Numbers

This is where most beginners mess up. Use: ARV, repairs, your calculator. If the deal works → proceed. If it doesn't → do not force it. Bad deals waste time.

Step 6: Make the Offer

Calm. Simple. Confident. You're not negotiating aggressively. You're explaining reality. Silence is okay. Pushback is normal.

Step 7: Contract or Follow-Up

Very few deals close on the first call. Your job: follow up, stay consistent, be easy to work with. Deals come from consistency, not pressure.

Step 8: Next Move

Once you have a real deal: Decide if it's a fit to move forward. Decide if you need guidance. Decide if JV makes sense. This is where access matters.
